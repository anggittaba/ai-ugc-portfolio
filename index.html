Process

Four steps, in order, every time. A pipeline — not a pile of experiments.

1

Research

Study what's winning in the niche — hooks, formats, pacing — before a single prompt is written. Nothing gets produced on a hunch.

2

Script

Draft hooks and scripts with ChatGPT, then rewrite into a native UGC voice. Ad-speak is the fastest way to get scrolled past.

3

Generate

Produce footage with Veo and Seedance, iterating prompts until faces, hands, and motion hold up to a frame-by-frame check.

4

Edit & ship

Cut in CapCut — captions, sound, pacing. Post, read retention, keep the winners, kill the losers, feed the learning back into step one.

On avoiding the AI look

The difference between content that converts and content that gets flagged as synthetic:

  • Imperfect on purpose. Slight camera shake, natural pauses, off-center framing — synthetic content fails the moment it's too clean.
  • Hands and faces get a frame-by-frame pass. Bad frames are regenerated, never patched.
  • Audio sells it. Realistic voice pacing and ambient sound do more for authenticity than any visual trick.
